# Coach's Training Manager
## Coaching Domain

This document defines the coaching concepts used throughout the application.
The software must reflect these definitions.

---

## Development Block

A major area of player development.

Examples

- Ball Mastery
- Receiving & Passing
- 1v1 Moves
- Speed
- Finishing
- Group Play

---

## Movement

A specific technical action performed by the player.

Examples

- Toe Taps
- Sole Rolls
- Pull Push
- Inside Cut
- Outside Cut
- Scissors
- Matthews

Movements belong to a Development Block.

---

## Drill

A structured activity used to teach one or more movements.

A drill should have:

- Purpose
- Setup
- Coaching Points
- Progressions
- Variations

---

## Development Outcomes

The improvements expected from repeated practice.

Examples

- Better ball control
- Improved first touch
- Confidence
- Balance
- Scanning
- Awareness
- Communication
- Decision making
