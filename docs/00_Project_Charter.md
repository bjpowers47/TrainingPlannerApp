# Training Planner Ap

## Project Charter

### Mission

Build better players by helping coaches coach better.

### Vision

Training Planner Ap is a player development platform that helps coaches plan purposeful training sessions, organize coaching knowledge, and support the long-term growth of every player.

The software exists to support coaching—not replace it.

### Guiding Philosophy

> "Progress is more important than perfection."
> — Bob Powers

Player development is a journey.

Every practice is an opportunity to move players forward.

The software should encourage growth, reflection, and purposeful coaching.

### Core Principles

1. Technology serves the coach.
2. Coaching philosophy drives the design.
3. Player development comes before administration.
4. Every practice has a purpose.
5. Observe often. Assess periodically.
6. Preserve coaching knowledge.
7. Progress is more important than perfection.
8. The coach is always the decision-maker.
9. The software asks thoughtful questions instead of giving coaching answers.
10. Build for the next ten years—not just the next release.

### Product Pillars

• Practice Builder
• Development Library
• Player Development
• Coach's Journal
• Season Development

### Success Metric

When a coach closes the application, they should feel more prepared, more organized, and more confident than when they opened it.

### Product Test

Whenever we finish a feature, we ask:

"Does this help a coach develop better players?"

If the answer is yes, it belongs.

If not, we redesign it.

Build Better Players. One Practice at a Time.
