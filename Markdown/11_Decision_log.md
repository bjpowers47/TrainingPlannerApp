Decision 001

Date: July 7, 2026

Decision:
Player Development is the primary purpose of the application.

Reason:
The software should support coaches in developing players rather than focusing primarily on administration.

Decision 002

Decision:
The application uses six permanent Development Blocks inspired by the Coerver methodology.

Reason:
They provide a consistent framework for organizing the Development Library and Practice Builder while remaining flexible enough for different coaching styles.

Decision 003

Decision:
Development Assessments track progress over time. The application does not use software-generated target ratings.

Reason:
The coach remains the decision-maker. The software's role is to document growth, not prescribe goals.

Decision 004

Decision:
The guiding philosophy is:

Progress is more important than perfection.

Reason:
This reflects the coaching philosophy behind the application and influences every design decision.
